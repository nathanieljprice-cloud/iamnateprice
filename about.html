About

Navy. Maine. Building from here.

Founder HQ for Nate Price, EnkiLabs, and the products that grew out of real operator work.

I served in the Navy, then rooted in Wells and Portland, Maine. The coast still sets the pace: quiet rooms, long horizons, work that has to hold up.

EnkiLabs is my Delaware S Corp. I own it outright. It is the home base for Gambit and SteadyPulse, and for the founder work that does not fit a single product label.

Before Gambit shipped as self-serve software, I ran competitive intelligence as a service shop. That chapter taught the craft. The products are what came next.
